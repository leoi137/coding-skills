# Law Currency Register

US privacy and consumer-contract law changes every legislative session and court term. This register lists the rules most likely to have moved since this skill was written. **The "lead" column is a starting point for a search, never authority.** Before a draft relies on a row, answer its question from a primary source during this job (statute or session-law site, the regulator's own page, the Federal Register, the court opinion). Law-firm alerts and IAPP/FPF trackers are good for finding the primary source. Vendor blogs are not sources.

Record each check in the fact ledger as `LAW: <topic> — <answer> — verified <date> — <URL>`. When an answer differs from the lead, draft to the answer and update the lead here. When sources conflict, draft to the more protective reading and list the conflict in the risk register.
