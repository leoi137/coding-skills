# Module: User-uploaded content

**Load when** users upload files, images, text or media that other people can see (including a user's counterparties, not just the public).

## Licenses
Users keep ownership; grant the company a license limited to hosting, storing, transmitting, displaying to the people the user chooses, and processing to operate the service. Add AI-processing in that license only if the product sends uploads to an AI provider. Users represent they have the rights to what they upload.

## Copyright — DMCA safe harbor (17 U.S.C. §512)
Safe harbor for user-stored content requires: a **designated agent registered with the U.S. Copyright Office** (online, renewed every three years), the agent's contact info on the site, a notice-and-takedown procedure, and a repeat-infringer termination policy that is actually implemented. If the agent isn't registered, either register it (cheap, quick — put it on the product-change list) or don't publish DMCA text implying safe-harbor status.

## Section 230 (47 U.S.C. §230)
Protects the company from most liability for third-party content and for good-faith moderation. It does not cover IP claims, federal criminal law, or the company's own content. The Terms should reserve the right (not the duty) to remove content and suspend accounts.

## TAKE IT DOWN Act (verify in `law-currency.md`)
A "covered platform" that hosts user-generated content for others must run a notice-and-removal process for nonconsensual intimate imagery and remove reported material within 48 hours. Products whose uploads are seen only by a specific counterparty (e.g., proof-of-work files to a client) are arguably outside "primarily provides a forum for user-generated content," but a simple reporting address costs nothing; include it if images/video are possible.

## Practical rules
Prohibit malware, unlawful content, others' personal data without authority, and content infringing IP. State storage limits and what happens to uploads when an account is deleted. Keep storage private by default and say so only if true (e.g., signed, expiring links).
